Acme Foundation: note on AI tools in the Berlin office
Version 1.0, 2 February 2026

This note applies to every AI tool used in the Foundation's Berlin office.

The Berlin team wrote its own principle in German: „Kein KI-Ergebnis verlässt das Haus ohne menschliche Prüfung.“

That principle stands beside this note and does not replace it.

The office manager must keep a list of approved AI tools and review it every six months.

Staff should ask the office manager before they try a new tool.

Questions about this note go to the office manager.
